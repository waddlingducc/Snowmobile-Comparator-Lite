## Media evidence and inventory

`media-permissions.csv` inventories **all 23 retained catalog reference images** imported by `src/data/snowmobiles.ts`. The `@assets` alias in `vite.config.ts` resolves to the repository's `attached_assets` directory. Each file existed and its byte size and SHA-256 were read locally on the audit date. These hashes establish which files were inventoried, not who created them or permission to use them.

Evidence found:

- Catalog imports map `norm_id01.jpg` through `norm_id23.jpg` to stable model IDs 1–23.
- The existing `docs/adsense-readiness.html` already states uploaded reference-image licensing and exact model/configuration matching were not independently established.
- Other uploaded image files have manufacturer/model-like or article-like filenames. No verified transformation/source chain linking them to every normalized retained file, original retrieval URL/date, copyright owner, license, permission document, or attribution requirement was found in the reviewed text records. Filename resemblance, public availability, and a manufacturer specification URL are **not** photo provenance or permission.
- CSV model/year fields describe the **catalog association**, not verified photographed identity. Renaming/correcting a catalog model does not make its retained photograph match that year, trim, track, region or optional equipment. This is especially important for corrected Arctic Cat and archived Yamaha entries.
- This is the 23-image catalog inventory, not a rights clearance of logos, favicons, social cards or every unused uploaded asset.

All 23 original-source URL, copyright-owner, license/publication-permission, attribution and pictured-configuration checks remain **unknown**. Retention was required by scope and is not a rights determination. No unauthorized media search/download or destructive replacement was undertaken.
